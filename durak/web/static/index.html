    Rules

    The last player holding cards loses.

    1. AttackLead with any card. Add cards matching a rank already on the table.
    2. DefendBeat each card with a higher card of the same suit, or a trump. Only a higher trump beats a trump.
    3. End the roundAfter a successful defence, choose End attack. The defender attacks next. If they take, select any throw-ins and choose Done; you attack again.
    4. Finish the gameHands refill to six while cards remain in the deck. Once it’s empty, get rid of yours. Both players finishing on the last attack and defence is a draw.
